Privacy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Print the Damn Book collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics and no advertising.

What the app does with your files

The app reads the page images in the folder you choose, on your PC, and sends the booklet to the printer you pick. Your images are never copied, uploaded or shared.

Settings

Your choices (paper size, two-sided mode and so on) are saved in a small settings file in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PrintTheDamnBook on your PC. Delete that folder to reset them.

Network access

The app makes no network connections. It has no update check and sends no telemetry.

This website

This site is static pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The download button takes you to GitHub, which has its own privacy policy.
