### Slice 12 — Android app (parked)

*Parked.* Lowest priority (user, 2026-10-22). Plan in detail before starting.

**Touches:** `src/PrintTheDamnBook.Mobile/**`, `src/PrintTheDamnBook.Core/**` (position-based classification and the print sequence, with tests), `tests/PrintTheDamnBook.Core.Tests/**`, `PrintTheDamnBook.slnx`, `.github/workflows/**`

**Why.** The same booklet printing from a phone or tablet.

**Direction** (user chose .NET MAUI, 2026-10-22):

- A .NET MAUI app that **reuses `PrintTheDamnBook.Core`** unchanged (imposition, page order, layout, manual
  two-sided plan). Pages are picked from the photo library or a folder.
- Printing goes through Android's print framework (`PrintManager`) with a PDF the app renders from the same
  sheet layout. Two-sided printing depends on the printer and the print service, so the manual two-sided flow
  (Slice 6) carries over: fronts PDF, reload prompt, backs PDF.
- The Nocturne look, adapted to MAUI; Google Play distribution.

**Open before planning:** whether page picking needs folder access or the photo picker is enough; and the Play
developer account.

#### Design (Redesign Directions mock, section t4 (frames 4a-4g), added 2026-10-23)

Seven frames in all; three are Android (4e Check as printed, 4f system print preview for pass 1, 4g reload prompt). The other four (4a-4d) are the iOS twin: layout, copy and logic are shared, only the native chrome differs. This subsection is the full record; Slice 13 points here. The prototype is `docs/design/MobileWizard.dc.html` (copy and logic in `renderVals()`, ~line 503). The mock agrees with the Direction above except for the open points below. The device bezels (`android-frame.jsx`, `ios-frame.jsx`: bezel, status bar, home indicator) are mock chrome, not app UI. The print preview in 4f is the system's screen, drawn to show what the app hands over; the app does not build it.

- **Shell (all steps).** Dark Nocturne page, 15px body text. Android top bar: back arrow (`ph ph-arrow-left`) on Check and Print, the small app icon (`icons/app-icon-sm.svg`) on Open, the title (`Print the damn book` on Open, then `Check`, `Print`, `Done`), and on Check a `ph ph-plus` "Add pages" button. Under it a 3-segment stepper (Open, Check, Print): done = `--color-accent-700`, current = `--color-accent` with a glow, todo = `--color-divider`. Scroll area padding 18px 20px 20px. The bottom bar holds the status line or the selection card, then the main button (primary, 50px high, 16px text). Main button copy: `Next: check the order`, `Next: printing`, `Print the damn book` (`ph ph-printer` in front; the other two end in `ph ph-arrow-right`). Footer line on Check: `Tap a page to move or remove it.`; otherwise the status, for example `12 pages on 3 sheets`.
- **Open, picking pages (12.2).** Heading `Point me at your pages` / `Cover, 1, 2, 3 … Back cover. I'll work out which page goes where on each sheet.` Drop card: `ph ph-images`, title `Pick your pages` (`Reading images…` with `ph ph-circle-notch` while loading), body `From Photos, pick the cover first and the back cover last. From a folder, I go by the names. JPG or PNG; I don't touch the originals.`, then tags `Cover` (accent), `1`, `2`, `3`, `…`, `Back cover` (accent). Two buttons, 48px, 15px text: primary `Choose photos` (`ph ph-images`), secondary `Choose a folder` (`ph ph-folder-open`). Rules: Photos keep **pick order**, first = cover, last = back cover; a folder is **sorted by name**, like Windows. Loaded: heading `Got your pages` / `Happy with these? Next you check the order.`, a card with the cover thumbnail, the source name (`12 photos` over `Photos · picked in order`, or the folder name over `Files › Downloads`), the status, and a secondary `Pick again` (`ph ph-arrows-clockwise`).
- **Check, Reading order (12.3).** Heading `Check the order` / `This is how the book reads. Tap a page, then tap where it should go.` Segmented control: `ph ph-book-open` `Reading order`, `ph ph-files` `As printed` (same labels as Windows). Warning chips (`ph-fill ph-warning`, warning colour `oklch(0.82 0.12 78)`, 14% fill): `{n} sheets is too thick to fold flat. 24 pages is about the limit.` (over 6 sheets), `{n} file has a name I don't recognise` / `{n} files have names I don't recognise`, `No Back cover file`. Spreads in a 2-column grid (gap 22px 16px) labelled `Front cover`, `Spread 1` …, `Back cover`. Tiles carry a `ph-fill ph-push-pin` on the cover and back cover; unrecognised files and the blank standing in for a missing back cover get a warning badge and a 2px warning ring. A selected tile has a 2px `--color-accent` ring with a glow; the cover dims to 50% while one is selected.
- **Reorder, tap-to-select, tap-to-place (12.4).** Tap a page to select it (cover, back cover and filler blanks select only when they carry a warning). Tap another tile: the selection moves to just before it. Tap the same tile again, or `ph ph-x`, to deselect. Tapping the cover does nothing; dropping on the back cover or a blank puts the page just before the back cover. This is `PageList.Move` / `MoveToEnd` / `IsPinned`.
- **Bottom bar with a selection (4b).** The status line becomes a card: 44px thumbnail, the page label, a one-line note, a round 44px `ph ph-trash` button (title `Remove from the book`) and a round 44px `ph ph-x` button (title `Done`). Notes: `Tap another page to move this one there.`; for an unrecognised file `Name not recognised, so it went to the end. Tap where it should go.` (warning colour); for the trailing blank `No “Back cover” file, so the back stays blank.`; `Filler: a sheet holds 4 pages.`; `Covers stay at the ends.`
- **Check, As printed (4e).** Sub-heading `This is what comes out of the printer. Fold the stack and it reads in order.` One card per sheet titled `Sheet N`, the first with an accent tag `test sheet + covers`; each card shows a `Front` and a `Back` face of two tiles. Read-only (no selection). Tiles come from Core `Sheet[]`.
- **Print, Paper and Two-sided (4c, 12.5).** Heading `Send it to the printer`. Sub-heading, Manual: `Two passes: I print the fronts, you flip the stack, I print the backs. {n} sheets of {paper}.`; Automatic: `One job, double-sided. {n} sheets of {paper}.` Stack card: `{n} sheets · {p} pages · {paper}` and `Two pages per side. The line down the middle is where you fold.` Fields in order: **Paper** (`A4` / `Letter`, A4 default); **Two-sided** (`Printer does it` / `I flip the stack`, **`I flip the stack` is the default**) with the hint `{printer} can't print two-sided, so I print the fronts, then ask you to put the stack back for the backs.` (Manual) or `Your printer flips the paper. Pick a printer that can do two-sided in the print sheet.` (Automatic); a switch `Print the cover sheet on its own` / `I'll pause after it so you can load thicker paper for the rest.`; a test card `First time with this printer?` with secondary `Print test sheet` (`ph ph-printer`) and text `Prints only Sheet 1 over two passes, with an arrow on the edge that feeds first, so you can check the back lines up.` (Manual) or `Prints only Sheet 1, so you can check the printer flips the paper the right way before you spend the rest.` (Automatic). After the test: `Test sheet sent. Is the back upside down? Switch Rotate backs 180° under More options.` (Manual) or `Test sheet sent. Flip it over: page 1 should sit behind the cover, the right way up. Upside down? Change Flip edge under More options.` (Automatic).
- **More options (4c).** Collapsible row, 48px: `ph ph-sliders-horizontal`, `More options`, a one-line summary (`flip the stack · fit · 5 mm · no page numbers`; `short edge` replaces `flip the stack` in Automatic; `numbers below` / `numbers above`), `ph ph-caret-down` (180 degrees when open). Contents: **Flip edge** (`Short edge` / `Long edge`, Automatic only); **`Reverse order of backs`** (`Turn on if the stack comes out face up.`, off) and **`Rotate backs 180°`** (`On by default: the same edge feeds first both times.`, on), Manual only; **Scaling** (`Fit` / `Fill and crop`, Fit); **Margin** (numeric, default `5`, suffix `mm`); **Page numbers** (`Off` / `Below` / `Above`, Off). They map to `FlipEdge`, `ManualDuplexOptions`, `ScalingMode`, `HalfSheetLayout.DefaultMarginMm` and `PageNumberPosition`.
- **System print hand-off, Android (4f, 12.7).** The app builds a PDF per pass and gives it to `PrintManager`. 4f shows the system screen: close `ph ph-x` (48px), printer name `Brother HL-L3220CW` (18px, `ph ph-caret-down`), rows `Copies` `1`, `Paper size` `A4`, `Two-sided` (`Off` in Manual, `Short edge` in Automatic), page previews captioned `Sheet N front` / `Sheet N back`, and a 64px print button (`ph-fill ph-printer`). Manual pass 1 shows only the fronts; pass 2 the backs, rotated 180 degrees when `Rotate backs 180°` is on, in reverse order when `Reverse order of backs` is on. Status line while sending: `Sending {job} to {printer}…`, then `Sent {job} to {printer}.` Job names: `the fronts`, `the backs`, `the test sheet fronts` / `backs`, `the test sheet`, `the cover front` / `back`, `the cover sheet`, `the rest of the booklet`, `the fronts of the rest`, `the backs of the rest`, `the booklet`. Cancelling pass 1: `Nothing was printed.` Cancelling pass 2: `The fronts printed, but the backs didn't. You cancelled the print sheet.`
- **Reload prompt (4g, 12.8).** An in-app Nocturne dialog between the passes. Title `Fronts are done`; body `Take the stack out, turn it printed side up and put it back in the tray as it is, same edge first. Then I print the backs.`; buttons `Cancel` (secondary), `Print the backs` (primary). Test variant: `Front of the test sheet is out` / `Put it back in the tray printed side up, arrow edge first. Then I print its back.` / `Print the back`. Cover variant: `Cover's away` / `The cover sheet has been sent. Load the paper for the inside pages, then tap OK to print the rest.` / `OK, print the rest`. Cancel: `The fronts printed, but the backs didn't. You chose not to continue.` (cover: `The cover sheet was sent, but the rest of the booklet was not printed. You chose not to continue.`).
- **Done (12.9).** Heading `Sent. Now fold the damn thing.` / `Three steps and it's a book.` Steps: `Grab the stack` (`Take all {n} sheets off the tray. Keep them in the order they came out.`), `Fold it in half` (`Fold the whole stack at once, cover on the outside. Run a thumbnail along the crease.`), `Staple the spine` (`Two staples on the fold. That's a book. Go read it to someone.`). Bottom bar: secondary `Print another copy` (`ph ph-printer`, back to Print), ghost `Make another book` (`ph ph-images`, back to Open). Status `Sent the backs to {printer}.`
