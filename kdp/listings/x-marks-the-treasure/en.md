## Description

Seagull is soaring over the harbour when she spots Captain Kathryn running for her ship, waving an old map. There is a little island on it and a big red X. X marks the treasure!

Up goes the sail and off sails the crew, with Seagull riding on the captain's big hat. But the trip is long, and one by one the pirates go quiet and grumbly. Billy only yawns. Izzy only sighs. Every tummy on board goes rumble, rumble, rumble! Can Seagull work out what is wrong in time to find the lost treasure?

A warm, bouncy adventure for ages 1 to 5 with plenty of "Squawk! Squawk! Squawk!" and "Yo ho, yo ho!" to join in with. It is a gentle bedtime read about teamwork, kindness and noticing what a friend needs, with a happy beach party at the end.
