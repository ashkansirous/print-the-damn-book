## Description

One sunny morning, Pancake Polly fishes a bottle out of the sea. Inside is a letter for Seagull: Granny Seagull and Grampy Seagull are coming to visit!

Seagull loves them so, so much. But they have not played together for a long, long time. What will she do with them? Soon a little plane lands beside the ship, and the games begin: a buzzing busy bee, Firefighter Granny with the ship's hose, Police Granny to the rescue, and a special sea-berry cake that only seagulls know how to make.

When it is time for Granny and Grampy to fly home, Seagull feels sad. Then the phone rings, and she learns that the people who love her are never really far away.

A warm, gentle read-aloud for ages 1 to 5 about grandparents, family love and missing someone, with plenty of "Squawk! Squawk! Squawk!" and "Mwah! Mwah!" to join in with. A comforting bedtime story for children whose grandparents live far away.
